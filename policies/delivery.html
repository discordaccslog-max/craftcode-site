Delivery policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

All CraftCode products are digital. We do not ship physical items, and there are no shipping charges.

How you receive your order

After your payment is confirmed, you receive a link to download your files. Depending on the checkout, the link is shown on the order confirmation page, sent to the email address you used at checkout, or both.

When

Usually within a few minutes of payment.

What you receive

One .zip file per product, containing a PDF lesson guide, Python (.py) exercise files with solutions, Python (.py) example programs, and a README.txt. The size of each download is listed on its product page. You'll need a way to open .zip files, which is built into most operating systems.

Didn't receive it?

Check your spam or promotions folder. If the link still hasn't arrived an hour after payment, email craftmail@codecraftprogram.com with your order number and we'll sort it out.

See the refund policy for what happens if a file is faulty or can't be accessed.
