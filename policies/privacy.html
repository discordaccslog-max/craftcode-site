Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: this website doesn't use accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We only handle personal information when you buy something or email us.

This website

This is a static website. It has no sign-up, no contact forms, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising tools. Like all web hosts, our hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as IP addresses and browser types to keep the service running and secure. That is covered by the hosting provider's own privacy policy.

When you buy something

Purchases are handled by our checkout provider, which collects the details needed to take payment, such as your name, email address, billing details and payment information, under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details (for example your name, email address, the product and the amount) so we can deliver your files, help with order problems, and keep the records required for tax and accounting. We do not receive or store your full card details.

When you email us

If you email craftmail@codecraftprogram.com, we use your email address and message to reply to you and to help with your order.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't share it, except with the service providers that help us run the business (such as payment and email providers) or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to provide support and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. We keep emails only as long as they're needed to resolve your request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how it's used. To make a request, email craftmail@codecraftprogram.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This website isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and its “last updated” date.
