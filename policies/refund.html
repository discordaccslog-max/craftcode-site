Refund policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: all CraftCode products are digital and delivered immediately after payment, so sales are final once your download link has been issued, except in the situations listed below.

When we will give a refund

We will refund your purchase (or, if you prefer, replace the files) if:

  • The files are faulty or you can't access them, and we can't fix the problem within 5 business days of you contacting us;
  • You were charged more than once for the same product by mistake;
  • We did not deliver the product you paid for; or
  • A refund is required by the consumer protection laws that apply to you.

When we will not give a refund

  • You changed your mind after the download link was issued.
  • You did not use the files, or did not finish the lessons.
  • You did not get a particular result. Learning outcomes depend on individual effort, prior experience and time invested, and no outcome is guaranteed.
  • The product's format (downloadable files, with no live instruction, video, tutoring or feedback) was not what you expected. The format is described on every product page, so please read it before buying.

How to request a refund

Email craftmail@codecraftprogram.com within 14 days of your purchase. Include your order number and a short description of the problem, for example a screenshot of an error message. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method. How long they take to appear depends on your payment provider and bank.

Customers in the EU and UK

Under EU and UK consumer law you normally have 14 days to cancel an online purchase. For digital content, that right ends once delivery begins, if you have agreed to immediate delivery and acknowledged that you lose the right to cancel. By completing your purchase, you request immediate access to the digital content and acknowledge that your right of withdrawal ends once the download link is issued. Your rights for faulty digital content are not affected.

Questions

If something is wrong with your order, please contact us first at craftmail@codecraftprogram.com. Most access problems can be fixed quickly.
